# Suggested reviewers — what to do if you don't know any

## Is it required?

**No.** "Suggested reviewers" is an Editorial Manager field, not a requirement
in Neurocomputing's Guide for Authors. You may leave it empty; the handling
editor will then pick reviewers from the editorial board and the literature.
Filling it is optional and only helps speed up handling — a bad or conflicted
list slows it down, which is why an empty field is better than a padded one.

## Rules if you do fill it

Suggest 3–5 people who satisfy **all** of the following:

* they work on interpretable ML, rule-based models, tabular learning, or
  scorecards — i.e. they can actually judge the paper;
* they are **not** at Sakarya University;
* they are **not** co-authors, collaborators, or co-grantees of either author;
* you have **no personal relationship** with them (no former supervisor, no
  close colleague, no friend);
* their e-mail is an **institutional address** you copied from the paper's own
  first page or their official staff page — never guess or reconstruct it.

**Verify each e-mail** from the first page of the cited paper, or from the
person's official university page. Do not use Gmail/Hotmail addresses, and do
not invent an address to fill the field.

## If you truly prefer to leave it empty

That is a valid choice. Just skip the step in Editorial Manager. Nothing is
rejected for an empty reviewer list.

## Also worth knowing

Editors may ignore your suggestions entirely, and they will never tell you who
reviewed the paper. So there is no benefit in trying to steer the choice — the
only thing that matters is that the names are qualified and unconflicted.
